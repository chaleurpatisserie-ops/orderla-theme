How these numbers are counted

A visitor is one phone or computer (remembered on that device). A visit ends after 30 minutes without activity. Orders sent counts taps on the final order button at checkout, so it can include orders that were never paid. Pages opened inside Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp or TikTok are counted under that app. Tracking started on 20 Nov 2026 around 23:45.
